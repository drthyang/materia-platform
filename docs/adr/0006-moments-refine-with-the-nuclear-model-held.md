# Moments refine with the nuclear model held, on every surface; a joint fit is opt-in and labelled

The moments of a powder magnetic model are refined by the moments fit (GLOSSARY.md): the moments with every nuclear row held, in rounds with the scale and the background, ending on a moments step, with esds that include those two rows. No step refines a moment and a nuclear row together. One module runs it (`core/magnetic/momentsFit.ts`), and it decides which rows each step refines, the stop rule, the cap, the iterations and the esds; a caller chooses none of them. The powder page's Refine moments, the Copilot's `refine_moments` and MCP's `refine_moments` all call it, so the three give the same answer for the same rows (to 1 part in 10⁹ on the known-truth case, `src/mcp/refineMoments.test.ts`). It holds the propagation vector: refining k belongs to a later, incommensurate stage with its own known-truth test.

A fit that frees moment-side rows (moment amplitudes, free k components) together with nuclear rows (every other row, the scale and the background included) is a joint fit. It is not MATERIA's method: one moments fit after a nuclear-only fit reads low, and a joint fit lets the scale, the ADPs and the positions trade with the moments while the esds hide it (docs/reports/2026-11-19-moments-alternation.md). Over MCP, `refine_magnetic_powder` refuses such a free set and says what to call instead. A caller who wants the joint fit all the same passes `joint: true`; the fit then runs, and its result carries a label (`note`, and `methodNote` on the refinement result) that the assessment and an export bundle repeat, so numbers from a joint fit cannot be quoted as the method's.

Decided by the owner on 2026-11-21 and 2026-11-22 (the architecture review's candidate 1). Not covered: the single-crystal and magnetic-PDF moment fits, which are one fit with the nuclear model held; the ranking's one fit per group, which at k = 0 frees the scale with the moments on the page; and the page's own Refine button, which still refines whatever rows the user frees.

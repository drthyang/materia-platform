# Compute irreps by chain induction, ship no tables

MATERIA builds the irreps of a space group at a commensurate k itself: projective small representations of the little co-group by deterministic induction along a chain of normal subgroups of index 2 or 3 (the route of Bilbao and spgrep, whose BSD-3 algorithm is ported, not its data), induced over the star to the full group, then reduced to physically irreducible form. The finite group G/T_k carries only the subgroup and isotropy layer. We rejected tabulated irreps (ISO-IR has no licence to redistribute; Bilbao's data are CC BY-NC-SA, which conflicts with AGPL-3.0) and Dixon–Schneider characters (no matrices, so no order-parameter directions). Deterministic induction gives stable bases, so order-parameter directions are reproducible. See docs/research/SUBGROUP_ENGINES.md §2 and §6.

Phase 5 (THY-36) put the magnetic page's representation analysis on this engine. It takes the small representations of G_k at any commensurate k as the top-left block of the engine's irreps induced from the user's arm (`complexIrrepsOfStar`). This replaces its ordinary irreps of an abelian little co-group, which were wrong on the zone boundary of a non-symmorphic group and missing for a non-abelian one at k ≠ 0.

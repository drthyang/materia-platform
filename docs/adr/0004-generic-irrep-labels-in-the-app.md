# Generic irrep labels in the app

The app labels irreps honestly and generically (the k-point, "irrep n of m", real or complex). Miller–Love (CDML) labels such as X3+ cannot be computed, only looked up in ISO-IR, which carries no licence to redistribute; they are matched only in tests and read from imported ISODISTORT mode CIFs. Revisit if BYU grants permission to bundle the labels.

Reaffirmed in phase 5 (THY-36, 2026-11-01). No licensed source of CDML labels was found: Bilbao, which computes its irreps, takes its labels from ISO-IR (docs/research/SUBGROUP_ENGINES.md §2.4), and the numbering is a table's convention that changes with the origin and the arm, not something the irreps determine. So the magnetic page takes the same generic labels as the Subgroups page, and its old "Γ1, Γ2, …" at every k, which read as CDML Γ labels, are gone. The opt-in ISOTROPY oracle (irrepLabelOracle.test.ts) checks, by character, that the generic labels stand for ISOTROPY's irreps one to one. The correspondence it prints stays in the CI log and is never committed (ADR 0005).

The numbering, fixed in THY-64 (2026-11-02). The n in "irrep n of m" is a total order of the irreps themselves (src/core/crystal/irreps/irrepOrder.ts): dimension, then reality (real, pseudoreal, complex), then the characters of the small representation on the rotations of the little co-group, each at its coset representative {W|w₀} (the smallest translation) and taken in a fixed order (the identity, the proper rotations, then the improper ones, each in row-major lexicographic order of the integer matrices), the larger real part first, then the larger imaginary part. It replaces the order the chain induction built the irreps in, which at a branch cut followed the sign of a rounding error, and the Γ chip's own order (characters printed to four decimals, compared with localeCompare); both differed between Node, Chrome and Safari, and between macOS and Linux. The Γ chip's Γn is now "Γ irrep n of m". The numbers still depend on the setting the operations are written in and on the arm of the star, as a table's do, and they are not ISOTROPY's; irrepLabels.golden.json pins them, and changing it is a deliberate re-baseline of model-visible labels.

# Generic irrep labels in the app

The app labels irreps honestly and generically (the k-point, "irrep n of m", real or complex). Miller–Love (CDML) labels such as X3+ cannot be computed, only looked up in ISO-IR, which carries no licence to redistribute; they are matched only in tests and read from imported ISODISTORT mode CIFs. Revisit if BYU grants permission to bundle the labels.

Reaffirmed in phase 5 (THY-36, 2026-11-01). No licensed source of CDML labels was found: Bilbao, which computes its irreps, takes its labels from ISO-IR (docs/research/SUBGROUP_ENGINES.md §2.4), and the numbering is a table's convention that changes with the origin and the arm, not something the irreps determine. So the magnetic page takes the same generic labels as the Subgroups page, and its old "Γ1, Γ2, …" at every k, which read as CDML Γ labels, are gone. The opt-in ISOTROPY oracle (irrepLabelOracle.test.ts) checks, by character, that the generic labels stand for ISOTROPY's irreps one to one. The correspondence it prints stays in the CI log and is never committed (ADR 0005).

The numbering, fixed in THY-64 (2026-11-02). The n in "irrep n of m" is a total order of the irreps themselves (src/core/crystal/irreps/irrepOrder.ts): dimension, then reality (real, pseudoreal, complex), then the characters of the small representation on the rotations of the little co-group, each at its coset representative {W|w₀} (the smallest translation) and taken in a fixed order (the identity, the proper rotations, then the improper ones, each in row-major lexicographic order of the integer matrices), the larger real part first, then the larger imaginary part. It replaces the order the chain induction built the irreps in, which at a branch cut followed the sign of a rounding error, and the Γ chip's own order (characters printed to four decimals, compared with localeCompare); both differed between Node, Chrome and Safari, and between macOS and Linux. The Γ chip's Γn is now "Γ irrep n of m". The numbers still depend on the setting the operations are written in and on the arm of the star, as a table's do, and they are not ISOTROPY's; irrepLabels.golden.json pins them, and changing it is a deliberate re-baseline of model-visible labels.

## Bilbao-consistent names (THY-73, 2026-11-08)

The owner asked for the Bilbao Crystallographic Server's names: KVEC's k-vector labels ("Δ (0, 0, ½)", "Σ (0, ½, ½)" on F-43m) and REPRES's irrep labels ("Δ1", "X5", "R4+"), each irrep under the name Bilbao gives the same irrep, matched by characters. Re-examined; this is an engineering reading of the licences, not legal advice.

**What Bilbao uses** (its static help pages, read 2026-11-08). KVEC names each k-vector type by the label and coefficients of CDML's Tables 3.9 and 3.11, gives the coefficients also in the reciprocal basis dual to the ITA conventional cell, derives the monoclinic unique-axis-b tables from CDML's unique-axis-c ones keeping CDML's labels, and adds indexed labels (H₀, H₁, …) only for equivalent representatives outside the zone ([brillouin_zone_databases](https://www.cryst.ehu.eus/cryst/help/brillouin_zone_databases.html), [examples_kvec](https://www.cryst.ehu.eus/cryst/help/examples_kvec.html)). REPRES computes its irreps, but "thanks to the collaboration of H. Stokes, the assigned irrep labels coincide with the notation used in Cracknell-Davies-Miller-Love tables and ISOTROPY" ([REPRES](https://www.cryst.ehu.eus/rep/repres.html)). The program pages carry the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 badge. The programs themselves (nph-kv-list, REPRES's output) answer a script with a Turnstile bot check, so their output can be read only by a person in a browser.

**Licences.**

- *Bilbao, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.* Nothing under it can go into MATERIA's code or bundle: AGPL-3.0 allows no further restrictions (§10), and NonCommercial is one; ShareAlike would put adaptations under BY-NC-SA itself. The licence reaches only what copyright or the EU database right protects (its §8(a)): facts, and insubstantial parts of the database, need no licence, which is why hand-typed values in tests are fine (ADR 0005). Bilbao is a Spanish university server, so the database right can apply: a table of labels for every group, taken from it, would need its permission.
- *ISO-IR and ISOTROPY state no licence* (both pages, checked 2026-11-08): no permission to redistribute. In the US, facts are free and a factual compilation is protected only in its selection and arrangement, but a complete numbering of every irrep is that compilation, and the numbers of the projective cases are the tables' own choices. Treated as needing permission.
- *CDML (1979) and Bradley & Cracknell (1972)* are copyrighted books. Their naming conventions, the names of points of the zone and of irreps, are not expression; their tables are.

**k-vector names: they ship.** A k-vector name is the name of a point, line or plane of the Brillouin zone in a published nomenclature (Bradley & Cracknell, CDML) that KVEC reproduces. A name with its coefficients is a fact, the whole set is a few hundred entries, and nothing is taken from Bilbao's database: its parameter ranges, Wyckoff blocks, figures and indexed labels are not used. What ships is a table written by hand, per Bravais lattice and for the arithmetic classes where CDML's names differ, of each special point's and line's label and representative coefficients in the conventional reciprocal basis of the ITA standard setting, with code that names a commensurate k by its star. It is checked in the opt-in ISOTROPY oracle against ISOTROPY's own k-point list (CDML's names) for every space-group type, and against KVEC values a person hand-types, with the page and date.

**Irrep labels: Bilbao's everywhere needs a table the app cannot licence.** The numbers are the CDML tables' convention; the irreps do not determine them (above). Two kinds of k differ.

- *Ordinary small representations*: every k of a symmorphic group, and Γ of any group. Here ISOTROPY's numbers behave like a rule. Each irrep takes the number of its point-group irrep in one fixed numbering per point group, with ± from the character of the inversion, and the little co-group oriented as CDML orients it at that k. The THY-64 oracle run ([41578511400](https://github.com/drthyang/materia-platform/actions/runs/41578511400)) is consistent with one numbering of 4/mmm at Pm-3m X and M, P4/mmm Z, I4/mmm M and Fm-3m X. Such a rule would ship as the labels of the 175 irreps of the 32 point groups and an orientation per k-vector type: small, and a nomenclature. But no source the project can open states either. The books are not at hand, and REPRES is behind its bot check. Both would have to be transcribed from ISOTROPY's output, which ADR 0005 does not allow. The rule's reach would rest on the oracle's sweep, not on CDML's text.
- *Projective small representations*: a non-symmorphic group on the zone boundary, such as P6₃/mmc A (the Mn₃Ga demo's point), P4₂/mnm Z, Fd-3m X and Pnma X. There is no rule. The numbers are the tables' choice and can only be looked up.

So Bilbao's irrep labels at every k mean shipping a label table taken from ISO-IR: every irrep of every group at every k-vector type, with enough characters to match the app's. That needs permission.

**Options** considered:

1. k-vector names now, irreps keep generic numbers with the name: "Δ irrep 2 of 4". No licence question.
2. Option 1, plus CDML labels by the point-group rule where the small representations are ordinary. The rule would be transcribed from ISOTROPY and swept against it in CI for every space-group type, with generic numbers elsewhere. It covers SrTiO₃ (Pm-3m) and GaTa₄Se₈ (F-43m), but not Mn₃Ga's A point, and it shows two label styles side by side. ADR 0005 would need amending.
3. Ask permission. H. T. Stokes and B. J. Campbell (BYU) would allow a compact label table generated from ISO-IR inside MATERIA under AGPL-3.0, with attribution. M. I. Aroyo (Bilbao) would be informed, since REPRES uses the same labels. With it, every irrep gets its Bilbao label, matched by characters when the table is built.
4. Bring your own table. The user downloads ISO-IR from BYU and the browser reads it locally, so nothing is redistributed. Complete, but clumsy (55 MB).

**Decision** (the owner, 2026-11-08): option 1 now, built so that better labels can be plugged in later. Option 3 stays open; the request below is the owner's to send.

**What shipped** (THY-73):

- *k-vector names* (kVectorNames.ts), written by hand from the Bradley & Cracknell / CDML convention for the cubic, tetragonal-P, orthorhombic-P and hexagonal lattices and the rhombohedral T, L and F, in the conventional reciprocal basis of the ITA standard setting (other settings are brought there first). A point has its name ("A"); a point on a line has the line's name and the coordinates of its star's canonical arm ("Δ (0, 0, 1/2)"), so the name does not depend on the arm and two stars on one line stay apart. As in CDML and KVEC, the holohedry's labels hold in every class of the lattice (F-43m's (0, ½, ½) is Σ); segments a lower class no longer relates to the holohedry's stay unnamed. Triclinic, monoclinic, centred orthorhombic and body-centred tetragonal lattices keep coordinates, because their CDML labels could not be written from the convention with confidence.
- *The check* (ADR 0005's wording kept): the opt-in ISOTROPY oracle compares every name with ISOTROPY's k-vector list for all 230 types. ISOTROPY is a check, not a source. Where it disagreed, the entry was removed, never corrected from it: Bradley & Cracknell's fcc Z (X–W) and orthorhombic E and P. Two further B&C lines it does not list, fcc S and bcc F, were withdrawn with them. The run after that: 2279 names agree, 0 disagree.
- *Irrep labels*: "<k-vector name> irrep n of m" in THY-64's order ("A irrep 1 of 3", "R irrep 8 of 10"); Γ keeps "Γn" on the Γ chip.
- *A label-provider seam* (irrepLabels.ts). Each irrep carries a fingerprint: the parent's setting, the star's name and k-vector label, the arm, the dimensions, the reality, and the small representation's characters on THY-64's fixed list of elements, rounded to 1e-3, in one key. The label shown is the active provider's, else the generic one. The default provider gives the generic labels. A table provider (`fingerprintTableProvider`), for a permitted CDML table or one built in the browser from a user's own ISO-IR file, matches by that key; an irrep it does not know keeps its generic label, and the engine, its numbering and saved projects (whose labels are text) do not change. No table ships. A test proves the seam with a two-row fake table. The Γ chip's "Γn" (displaciveModes.ts) is outside the seam: a table provider would add one call there.

**The permission request** (draft, for the owner to send to H. T. Stokes and B. J. Campbell, BYU, copying M. I. Aroyo, Bilbao): "MATERIA (github.com/drthyang/materia-platform, AGPL-3.0) computes space-group irreps itself and would like to show the CDML labels that ISO-IR and Bilbao's REPRES use. May we ship a compact table generated from ISO-IR, giving labels and the characters needed to match them, inside the AGPL app, citing ISO-IR and Stokes, Campbell & Cordes (2013)?"

# Can lines: do the sample's peaks sit on the V or Al can's lines?

The owner's request: "We are doing neutron diffraction, add the feature that I
can check if the diffraction signals overlap to Vanadium or Aluminum can
background." The powder page now draws the vanadium and aluminium can lines
on the pattern and lists, in the Validation view, every sample reflection and
observed peak within one FWHM of a can line. Neutron data only (constant
wavelength and time of flight). Nothing is fitted or excluded, and nothing of
it reaches the Copilot (no tool, no `get_state` field, no model-visible text).

## What the page shows

- **A "Can lines" toggle** in the plot's toolbar, beside the phase ticks. Two
  tick rows join the phase rows: **V can** and **Al can**. Each tick is a
  faint full-height mark (where the line is) and a solid bar up from the
  row's foot at the line's true relative strength per volume of metal, so the
  vanadium lines are drawn as weak as they are: on the Mn₃Ga POWGEN demo V's
  strongest line is about 1/40 of Al's. On X-ray data the toggle is absent.
- **Each can's d-scale** under the plot (default 1): the lines sit at the
  room-temperature cell's d times it. Beside a scale other than 1, the shift
  it makes at the can's strongest line in the range, in FWHM of the page's
  profile there. When the data file states the sample temperature, a button
  offers the cooled value from the tabulated thermal expansion, with the
  shift that value makes (for a stated 100 K on NOMAD bank 4: V × 0.99864,
  211 −0.2 FWHM; Al × 0.99629, 311 −0.6 FWHM). No temperature is assumed:
  a number without a unit, a sample name (`…_at_300K`) or a value at or
  below 0 K (the NOMAD reduction's placeholder "at temperature 0.0 K") does
  not count, and two different temperatures in one file give none.
- **A "Can lines" section in Validation**: one line first (how many sample
  reflections and observed peaks lie within 1 FWHM of a can line), the
  d-scales, then two lists, the strongest can lines first. Each row gives the
  can line (phase, hkl, d), its relative intensity among that can's lines,
  the sample reflection (reflections of one phase at one d as one row, e.g.
  822/660) or the observed peak, the separation in FWHM, and a plain reading:
  "The Al 311 line at 1.221 Å sits under the sample's 822/660 (1.223 Å): its
  intensity may include the can." An observed peak with no sample reflection
  within 1 FWHM reads "… it may be the can's". **view →** opens the pattern
  there with the can rows on.

## How each decision is implemented

- **Data-driven cans** (`src/core/diagnostics/canLines.ts`, `CAN_PHASES`):
  each entry names a phase of the known-phase library
  (`knownPhases.ts`: Al Fm-3m a 4.0495 Å, V Im-3m a 3.0240 Å, room
  temperature) and optionally an expansion table. Cu, steel or Nb is one more
  entry (Cu, α-Fe, austenitic steel and Nb are already in the library); the
  plot colour falls back to a neutral one for a can without its own.
- **(a) Lattice.** d = d₂₉₃ × s per can, s stated and adjustable. The cooled
  value is a(T)/a(293 K) = 1 + ΔL/L₀(T), interpolated in the cited table as a
  cubic Hermite with α as the slope, the slopes limited (Fritsch–Carlson) so
  the curve never turns back between rows; no value outside the table's
  range. The library cells are taken as 293 K values (the 5 K to 298 K moves
  Al's d by 1·10⁻⁴).
- **The expansion data**: Y. S. Touloukian, R. K. Kirby, R. E. Taylor and
  P. D. Desai, *Thermophysical Properties of Matter*, Vol. 12: *Thermal
  Expansion, Metallic Elements and Alloys* (IFI/Plenum, New York, 1975),
  recommended values, Table 1R (Al, 5–900 K, ±3 %) and Table 60R (V,
  5–2000 K; ±10 % below 293 K, provisional there; ±5 % above), copied from
  the printed tables (DTIC AD-A129115). Al cross-checked against
  Corruccini and Gniewek, *Thermal Expansion of Technical Solids at Low
  Temperatures*, NBS Monograph 29 (1961), Table 2.1.
- **(b) Overlap** = within one FWHM of the page's own profile at the can
  line's d. The profile is the page's isotropic one (instrument terms and the
  isotropic sample terms, `placePeaks` without hkl), constant wavelength or
  time of flight; its apex and FWHM are measured on the shape as drawn (the
  apex where the slope changes sign, each half-maximum crossing by
  bisection), so FCJ asymmetry and back-to-back exponentials are measured, not
  approximated. Sample reflections are placed the same way, observed peaks
  by a parabola through ln y at the local maximum (exact for a Gaussian).
  The separation |x − x_can| / FWHM_can is printed to 0.01, and one printed
  1.00 counts as within.
- **(c) UI**: the toggle in the plot toolbar, the tick heights through an
  optional `height` on a reflection tick (drawn by the plot only when set, so
  every other tick row is drawn as before), the scale row under the plot and
  in the section, the section after the Validation view
  (`src/app/ui/CanLines.tsx`). The plot receives the can rows only while the
  toggle is on; the report's and the Agent's tick lists are untouched.
- **Intensities**: the can phase's |F|² with the engine's neutron coherent
  scattering lengths (V −0.3824 fm, Al 3.449 fm), its multiplicity, the
  page's Lorentz factor (on or off as the page) and intensity corrections
  (`placePeaks`). Relative within each can; across cans per unit volume
  (÷ V_cell²) for the tick heights. The section says the can's scale against
  the sample is unknown.

## Tests

`src/core/diagnostics/canLines.test.ts` (11 tests) and
`src/parsers/statedTemperature.test.ts` (3 tests), under a second together.

- Positions against an independent calculation written in the test
  (d = a/√(h²+k²+l²), fcc all-odd/all-even, bcc h+k+l even): every V and Al
  line and only those, at scale 1 and 0.9958, on constant wavelength
  (λ 1.5 Å, the ticks at 2 asin(λ/2d), a Gaussian's apex there to 10⁻⁸°) and
  time of flight (difC 5000, the ticks at difC·d, the apex within half a
  FWHM of it). A Gaussian's measured FWHM is its Caglioti width to 10⁻⁹.
- The overlap table on a synthetic pattern: sample reflections planted 0.5,
  1 and 2 FWHM from Al 111 give the first two, printed 0.50 and 1.00; three
  Gaussian peaks planted 0.5 FWHM from Al 111, 1 FWHM from Al 200 and 2 FWHM
  from Al 220 give the first two with those separations and their readings.
- The cooled d-scale at the cited rows exactly (Al 50, 100, 200, 293, 500 K;
  V 25, 100, 200 K), constant below 5 K, none past the table; between rows
  within 4·10⁻⁵ of NBS Monograph 29 at 90, 140, 180, 240 and 273 K and
  monotonic; the shift in FWHM against 2θ computed in the test.
- V against Al: per atom, I/(m·d⁴·DW·n²) is one number within each can and
  V/Al = (0.3824/3.449)² = 0.0123 to 10⁻⁹; V's strongest tick under 0.05 of
  Al's.
- The stated temperature: header lines in K and °C, a CIF's ambient
  temperature, the NOMAD header's 0.0 K placeholder and `_at_300K` name not
  taken, two temperatures give none.

## Browser check

At 1600, 1280, iPad (820) and phone (390) on the Mn₃Ga POWGEN demo: the
toggle, the rows, the scale row and the section, no horizontal overflow, no
page errors (the section is a table above 560 px, rows that wrap below). On
the published NOMAD GaTa₄Se₈ 300 K data (bank 4, with the NOMAD 2023A
instrument file; local only, not in the repository) the section lists 91
sample reflections and 35 observed peaks, Al 311 first (0.29 FWHM from the
sample's 822/660); the file states no temperature, so no cooled value is
offered. A test copy of that file with its header changed to 100 K offered
V × 0.99864 and Al × 0.99629 (the table's values). On an X-ray page the
toggle and the section are absent.

## Unchanged

Every scripted eval run (Copilot text, `get_state`, `assess_refinement`, the
eval pages) is identical to the last digit before and after (full-precision
dump of `main` and the branch). No engine function changed; the plot draws
every existing tick row as before.

## Limits

- The can's own line widths are taken as the page's profile: a can's
  microstructure differs from the sample's.
- Observed peaks are local maxima 5σ above their ±4-point window: a can line
  hidden in a sample peak's flank is not a separate peak; the sample-reflection
  list is the check for it.
- A part away from the sample position shifts its lines by more than the
  cooled scale; the scale is the user's to set.
- Only the stated temperature is read from the file; a temperature in a
  separate log or a sample name is not.

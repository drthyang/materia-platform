## Evidence

- **`core/math/extent.test.ts`**: `minMax` on a list and an empty one;
  500 000 values, which `Math.min(...)` throws on; a 2θ scan of 500 000
  points drawn by `powderCurves`; a 500 000-point TOF pattern's d range. The
  last two fail on `main` (Maximum call stack size exceeded).
- **Nothing else changed**: the suite passes as it did; the only tests
  removed are the two that pinned the copies (`scTools.extent`, the plot's
  `extent`), whose assertions `extent.test.ts` carries.

## Found, not fixed

The survey also found copies that do not behave alike. Each changes a
number or a result somewhere, so each is its own change (suggested as
separate tasks where marked):

- **The free globs** on MCP do not try an id without its multi-phase prefix
  (`p0_`), as the app's do: `pos_*` frees nothing on a two-phase MCP
  parameter set (task).
- **The per-point σ** when a file gives none: the page's live wR falls back to
  √o, the diagnostics to √max(y, 1), and two tools take σ = 0 as given (task).
- **The compute client's** powder, PDF and single-crystal multi-starts still
  write their own pool-or-thread runner, beside `strategy.ts`'s `Solver`;
  the single-crystal path and MCP keep Jacobian rules of their own (task).
- Smaller copies left as they are: the Messages-API stream adapters
  (`openaiCompat.ts`, `ollamaNative.ts`), the MCP host's and executor's
  result view and `read_ref` window, the hkl and twin-law helpers of the
  single-crystal page, median and quantile, the seeded noise of the test
  pages, `labelOf`, `isCell`, the percent-as-text of the workbenches, the
  SC and PDF page models' `withFixed`/`withReset`, the "free row" predicate
  (47 inline copies), and the simulator's use of `testSupport`'s
  `sessionCurves`, which differs from the page's curves.
